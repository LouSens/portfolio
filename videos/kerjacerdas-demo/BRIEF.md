## Intent

A silent app walkthrough for the KerjaCerdas case study on the portfolio site. It should show the
real product doing its job in the order a job seeker meets it: pick a target, see what is missing,
learn it, apply, and then the employer's side. It autoplays muted and loops inside the case study,
like the pickleball clip already on the site.

## Assets

- ../../static/screenshots/kerjacerdas-v2/landing.webp — real landing page; the opening beat.
- ../../static/screenshots/kerjacerdas-v2/seeker-dashboard.webp — real seeker dashboard; ranked matches.
- ../../static/screenshots/kerjacerdas-v2/seeker-match.webp — real match results; the missing-skill flags.
- ../../static/screenshots/kerjacerdas-v2/seeker-learning-plan.webp — real learning plan; the gap being closed.
- ../../static/screenshots/kerjacerdas-v2/seeker-applications.webp — real applications list; rejection reasons.
- ../../static/screenshots/kerjacerdas-v2/employer-applicants.webp — real employer view; ranked applicants and skill map.

## Customizations

- On-screen captions in English, one short line per beat. No voiceover, no music, no sound.
- The screens are the real app (its UI text is Indonesian); captions carry the meaning in English.

## Notes

- Only real captured screens. No rebuilt or invented UI, no fake cursor interactions.
- Must read on a phone-width embed: captions large, one focus per beat.
- Seamless enough to loop: the close hands back to the opening.
