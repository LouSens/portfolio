NeuralVoid interface storyboard v2

A redesign for people who are not technical: someone uploads their TikTok watch history and reads, in ordinary words, how much they watch, when, and what to try. The look is the dark and orange concept design from the portfolio. Numbers shown are examples.

10 screens · plain language · works on a phone
New in v2: from "interesting" to "useful"
  • Why: charts make someone say "huh". They come back for an app that says what the habit costs them, what they would get back, and helps them change one thing.
  • What it adds up to (screen 04): the hours turned into things people feel, and the nights that ran past midnight.
  • Your plan (screens 07 and 08): pick one change, see the hours it gives back each month, then get a nightly reminder and the steps to set TikTok's own limit.
  • Since last time (screen 09): upload again in two weeks and see what changed. Only a few numbers are kept, in the person's own browser.
  • Save or share (screen 10): a picture of the summary to keep, send to a friend, or show a parent or counsellor.
  • Your own clock: hours are shown in the visitor's time zone, so "11 pm" means their 11 pm.

WORD LISTapplies to every screen

Rule: if a word needs explaining, it is replaced or gets a sentence under it. No model names, feature names or percentages of confidence on the main screens.

What changes in the code

  • Front end: the screens above replace the current landing page, upload panel and three-tab dashboard in frontend/src/App.jsx.
  • Analysis: the scoring is unchanged. The backend returns a few extra numbers the new screens need (nights past midnight, late-night hours, the length of each sitting) and reads hours in the visitor's time zone.
  • Example mode: "See an example first" keeps working without a file, using made-up numbers labelled as an example.
  • Written summary: one small backend change. The instruction that asks for a "clinical" three-part assessment is reworded to ask for plain language under the three new headings.
  • Phones: tabs become a scrolling row, cards stack in one column, and the hour-by-hour picture scrolls sideways.
  • Kept on the device: "Since last time" stores a handful of totals in the browser, with a button to forget them. No accounts.
  • Not included: accounts, syncing between devices, or live tracking of the phone.
